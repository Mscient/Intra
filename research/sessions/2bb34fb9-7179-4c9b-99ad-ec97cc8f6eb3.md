## Agreed contract

Settlement occurs only after the shared final complete session, for an explicit unique instrument list. Exactly one sourced event is required per selected contract, including flat contracts. Each event closes the full signed residual position; partial fills and prior exits affect the actual residual, not a requested settlement quantity. Charges and GST use caller-declared rounding. Independent expiries and sliced settlements are excluded.
