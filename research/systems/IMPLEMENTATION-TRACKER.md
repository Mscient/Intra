Partial mode caps each fill at displayed top size rounded down to whole market lots, cancels the IOC remainder, and accounts for partial position basis, fees, and checkpoint recovery. Neither L1 mode models queue priority. The quote journal hash participates in run identity and independent reconciliation reselects quote references and verifies filled quantity. Position handling supports `ALLOW_OVERNIGHT`, fail-closed `REQUIRE_FLAT`, and an optional session-end exit under the selected execution model; that policy still requires review.

Expiry stitching, index-to-futures substitution, passive-touch fills, and live order egress are not supported.

- Preserve dated futures/options contracts and master snapshots. Do not stitch expiries or map an index candle to a contract fill.
