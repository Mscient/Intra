## Executive finding

Two previously provisional implementation dates can now be pinned to primary circulars: CAS-related equity-derivatives changes went live on **2026-08-03** (FAOP75472, p1); the revised derivatives pre-open went live on **2026-09-07** (FAOP76186, p1). The underlying detailed FAOP74467 and FAOP74970 documents remained inaccessible in this research tool. Lot and expiry changes are contract-generation/contract-maturity rules, not single calendar-wide replacements. Source hashes were not invented; this report links documents and pinpoints without attaching downloaded exchange content.

## Questions 1–6

### 1. Sessions, pre-open and special sessions

The current [CAS page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/closing-auction-session), under “Session Timings,” shows equity derivatives 09:15–15:40 IST. CAS itself is a cash-stock session, initially for stocks with derivative contracts: its stock auction is not a separate NIFTY futures/options auction. The page's 2026-08-12 update is not an effective date. FAOP75472 p1 establishes the change's live date; full normal-session trade-modification cutoff and derivative closing-price rules still need FAOP74467 or the current detailed FAQ. **Do not use FAOP75472's mock-session 15:50 cutoff as live evidence.**

The current [derivatives pre-open page](https://www.nseindia.com/static/products-services/equity-derivatives-pre-open-session), “Session Timings,” “Eligible Contracts,” and “Additional Note,” documents the current phases and eligibility. The concise source transcription below contains the supported mechanics; the 2026-09-04 page-update date is distinct from the 2026-09-07 go-live notice.

| Current-page field | Observed value / scope |

| Eligibility | Current-month index/stock futures; next-month futures during last five trading days before current-month expiry. Options excluded by stated scope. |
| Entry | 09:00–09:05 market/limit entry, modification, cancellation; 09:05–09:10 limit-only; random closure in final two minutes. |
| Matching / buffer | Matching begins immediately after collection closes, nominal 09:10–09:12; buffer 09:12–09:15. |
| Algo market exception | Allowed during first five minutes; later market requests rejected. |

The original 09:08 collection schedule is retained in the earlier project review, but FAOP71092 could not be reopened here. FAOP71582 p1 independently confirms pre-open **order-log indicators** effective 2025-12-08; that alone does not prove every original trading phase or first trading date. The updated regime date is no longer merely a secondary lead; its complete timing transition still requires the detailed implementation circular. No blanket historical 09:15–15:30 regime is fabricated.

### 2. Expiry, holidays and transition periods

FAOP68747 p1–2 partially modifies FAOP68685. Its NIFTY table changes weekly Thursday to Tuesday and monthly/quarterly/half-yearly last Thursday to last Tuesday. Existing contracts expiring on/before 2025-08-31 remain unchanged; newly generated expiries from 2025-09-01 use Tuesday from the 2025-06-26 EOD generation boundary. Existing long-dated NIFTY options are realigned at 2025-07-31 EOD. These are different events and must remain different data fields. The annexure explicitly includes the 2025-09-02 first illustrated new weekly expiry and 2025-09-25 → 2025-09-30 long-dated realignment. Settlement schedules are to be supplied separately by clearing corporations.

Current contract specifications prescribe previous trading day if Tuesday is a trading holiday. That is a rule, not a verified holiday calendar. A selected contract's expiry must resolve against its dated master and holiday/special-session notices. Do not derive final trade time, settlement observation instant, or cash payment time solely from the expiry weekday.

### 4. Auction mechanics

The current pre-open page's “Order Matching Period,” “Determination of Equilibrium / Opening Price,” and “Unmatched Orders” establish single-price matching: maximize executable volume, then minimize imbalance, then nearest previous close; midpoint tie uses previous close. Both-side market-only orders match at base; if no price is discovered, first normal-market trade sets open. Sequence: market/market, residual market/limit, then limit/limit, with stated time or price-time priority. Unmatched limits retain timestamps; market residuals become limits at discovered equilibrium.

**Unresolved boundary:** how unmatched market orders are released when *no equilibrium exists* cannot be filled in by combining the two statements. No-equilibrium/no-trade/residual cases need the detailed dated rule. Nor does this page provide historical random-close realizations: simulation must use an explicitly synthetic/seeded closure time unless an authorized observed time exists. `CallAuctionEngine` is a generic primitive; phase admission, carry policy, reference price and source regime must be separately supplied. Current-page mechanics are not retroactive rules for every pre-open date.

### 5. Close and settlement

[NSE Clearing settlement-price table](https://www.nseclearing.in/clearing-settlement/equity-derivatives/settlement-price), updated 2024-05-07, states index final settlement uses the relevant underlying index close on last trading day, subject to authority override; daily futures settlement uses last-30-minute VWAP across exchanges or authority-determined price. This is an official current page, **not a post-CAS effective-date amendment chain**. The CAS page confirms an indicative index is disseminated, but gives no SmartAPI candle-source mapping.

[Settlement mechanism](https://www.nseclearing.in/clearing-settlement/equity-derivatives/settlement-mechanism) describes automatic European ITM exercise and cash settlement, with index final payment on T+1. [Settlement schedule](https://www.nseclearing.in/clearing-settlement/equity-derivatives/settlement-schedule) says T+1 working day and clearing-member pay-in by 09:00. These are clearing obligations, not the owner's account credit timestamp. Dated cash-calendar/settlement notices remain required.

**Conflict:** current NIFTY product introduction says “physical settled” while NSE Clearing's index exercise text describes cash settlement. Do not propagate the landing-page wording into a settlement registry. See DR-09's detailed convention conflict. Also do not assume the derivative last traded premium, final futures quote, indicative index and official index settlement value are interchangeable. The exact CAS-era index close calculation, fallback and publication/finality time remain unresolved here.

## Candidate schema and coding consequences

The CSV is a **source event timeline**, intentionally incompatible with the production market-rule CSV. Every row carries `production_eligible=false` and remains pending independent review. Its `event_date` is interpreted only with `event_boundary` and `contract_selector`; `effective_through_status=UNKNOWN` cannot mean “valid forever.” It contains no invented row for an unverified date and no implied 24-hour boundary for EOD events. `source_access_date` is this review, while `source_publication_date` is the issuer date. No current-page field receives an effective date from that page's update date. INR tick units, underlying units/lot, Asia/Kolkata local time and generation-versus-trade-date semantics are explicit.

Relevant current destinations: `data/MarketRuleRegimeSchedule.java`, `data/SessionCalendarCsvReader.java`, `data/InstrumentMasterCsvReader.java`, `reference/InstrumentMasterSnapshot.java`, `book/CallAuctionEngine.java`, plus settlement inputs. These are source-input consumers; a passing source-reference/hash check does not authenticate a circular. Before mapping research into them:

1. Resolve each calendar day, dated contract and rule dimension independently; preserve generation time, applicability date, original expiry and revised expiry. Split old/new lot cohorts that coexist on one date.
2. Compile a separately reviewed registry with field-level citations, reviewer ID/date and supersession decision. Do not label an entire composite regime VERIFIED because its go-live date was confirmed.
3. Keep trade-date, order-entry phase, final trading cutoff, index observation cutoff, price publication/finality and clearing payment date separate.
4. Test actual daily tick/lot master values against the governing scheme without replacing the master with a live-price heuristic.
5. Block auction replay without dated eligibility, phase, reference and residual policy; preserve no-equilibrium as an unresolved state.

Suggested negative vectors (synthetic, not exchange observations): different NIFTY expiries straddling first-new-lot cohorts on one date; long-dated July31 expiry remap applied before versus after EOD; prior-month index exactly 15,000/30,000 versus just above; intraday crossing that must not reset tick; an option incorrectly admitted to futures pre-open; market request after permitted phase; market-only/no-equilibrium residual; holiday Tuesday with unverified prior trading day; mock date mistakenly imported as regular-session hours; 15:40 futures quote incorrectly substituted for official index final value. Production test expectations require reviewer-accepted sources first.
