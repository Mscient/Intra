# DR-04 — Historical data availability, licensing and research corpus

Researcher: Codex, API/data assignment. Research/access date: **2026-11-05**, Asia/Kolkata. No acquisition or purchase was performed.**

## Executive finding

Official historical order/trade, index and master products exist, but no product agreement or authorized corpus was supplied. Public accessibility does not permit simulator use. NSE Clearing's website terms expressly restrict virtual-trading/simulation use; NSE's separate non-display policy covers algorithmic/internal analysis and requires its own agreement framework. An owner's broker subscription cannot be assumed to cover those rights.

The current NSE historical specification is **v1.19 dated 3 August 2026**, superseding the v1.17 reference initially found in DR-08/10. It distinguishes trimmed from full records and delayed full-data delivery. This matters for recent-date gap investigation and order reconstruction. A vendor “tick” label can mean one-second samples, and a continuous daily series is not dated-contract executable history.

D2 delivery timing is provisional: §8.3 p72 gives next-day 01:00 IST for F&O SFTP and previous-three-day file availability; §8.1 pp70–71 distinguishes delayed full files from daily trimmed files. This is **server availability**, not permission to retain downloaded data for three days or indefinitely. A recent target may lack full history even if trimmed data exist. Do not infer that an EOD product includes all fields of delayed full history.

Expired-contract feasibility: the NSE F&O historical format can identify individually dated instruments and the catalog offers historical F&O. Complete NIFTY expired futures/options coverage for the owner's chosen date span is **not established without a catalog response and permitted sample**. API request-window limits are not archive start dates. Never construct a nonexistent contract simply because a strike/expiry can be generated algorithmically.

Roll consequence (**INFERENCE**): a back-adjusted/ratio-adjusted or stitched continuous price may remove the actual price difference between contracts. It cannot provide executable prices, dated multiplier, margin, fees or settlement for either leg. Keep raw dated contracts, a separately versioned causal roll rule, actual two-leg timestamps/costs and explicit gaps. A continuous series may serve a declared signal feature; it cannot replace fill prices or cash ledgers.

Separate exchange trigger/checksum from local SHA-256 and from authenticity evidence. A hash validates byte identity, not publisher authority, complete stream delivery, legal completeness or permission to use a dataset. An expiry of portal download availability is separate from end-user retention/deletion.

Proposed validation conditions: no corpus promotion with unknown/prohibited intended use; reject unidentified continuous data from executable-contract slot; no full-schema parser for trim file; no current-master historical join; no “tick” event-complete label on sampled observations; preserve revision lineage and missing dates; reject UTC conversion while D2 conflict unresolved; no restricted raw data in Git. These are design recommendations, not executed acquisition or new code.
