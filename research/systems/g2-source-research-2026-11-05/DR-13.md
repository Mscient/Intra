## Q1 — Proposed seven-layer control map

This is an explicit proposed allocation of the plan's seven layers, not a recovered quotation of unavailable original §9.3 labels. None of the layers relies solely on a strategy's self-reported risk. Higher-layer permission cannot override a lower-layer block on increasing risk. A separate emergency-control lane is described in Q5.

| Proposed layer | Purpose and independent state source | Inputs → decision / latch | Failure mode | Clearance authority / evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0 — intent validity | Pure validator; dated approved reference service | Contract, units, tick, permitted session/type, policy version → valid or reject | Missing identity/rules, overflow, malformed or unsupported order | Corrected proposal under reviewed policy; immutable rejection/check report |
| L1 — strategy envelope | Gateway counters independent of strategy | Strategy allocation, accepted/reserved exposure, ordered proposals → allocate or block | Strategy exceeds allocation or uses stale configuration | Authorized risk owner changes version; allocation ledger and approval |
| L2 — portfolio/account risk | Independently ingested executions, positions, marks and margin evidence | Worst-case positions, working risk, P&L, stress, concentration → reserve/reject; hard-loss latch | Missing source, mismatch, unsupported model, cap breached | Human incident review and independently reconciled state; risk report |
| L3 — egress enforcement | Separate process owns final permission checks and writer/fencing lease | Durable reservation token, rate/collar/session scope, token freshness → single-use permit or reject | Gateway timeout, stale token, bypass attempt, rate exhaustion | Healthy lease and re-evaluation; decision/dispatch/ack audit; no transport implemented here |
| L4 — watchdog and kill | Separate failure domain, direct heartbeat/journal/coverage evidence | Liveness, age, reconciliation, incident severity → K0/K1/K2/K3 | Hung strategy/gateway, journal loss, compromised process | K-specific human clearance; independent trigger/action observations |
| L5 — broker/exchange controls | Broker/exchange as independent external authority | Accepted/rejected order status, margin limits, session/terminal restrictions → external decision | Broker rejects, disconnects or executes while local state is uncertain | Reconcile official external evidence; local controls cannot override broker state |
| L6 — human governance/manual path | Authorized operator on separately available path | Incident, residual risk, release/config identity → stop/clear/release | Operator unavailable, compromised access, unreviewed exception | Authenticated role and evidence-bound approval; no agent holds this authority |

The exact L0–L6 names are an owner/reviewer input. This proposed mapping can be adopted or mapped to the original design without altering the invariants below.

## Q2 — Signed execution accounting and independent reconciliation

Canonical position unit is **underlying contract units**, with an explicit positive `units_per_lot` and settlement multiplier. Existing gateway quantities are whole contracts with a supplied multiplier; an adapter must explicitly convert them. Never mix lots, contract units, option premium rupees and notional rupees in a single long field.

For instrument `i`, signed position is `q_i = opening_units_i + Σ active_execution_signed_units_i + Σ authorized_position_adjustments_i`. BUY is positive, SELL negative. Every execution is identified by `(source, nonidentifying account scope, venue, trade date/session namespace, execution_id)` and retains order identity, contract version, economic time, received time, source sequence, price, units, fees and provenance. Processing identity is not the same as business execution identity.

Exact duplicate payload is a no-op. Conflicting reuse is quarantined and halts increasing risk. An explicit correction references its prior economic execution and version: reverse the old contribution, apply the corrected one atomically, retain both audit records, and re-evaluate every limit. A bust removes the prior economic contribution using its explicit reference; it does not invent an opposite market trade. Unknown original/correction ordering remains unresolved until source causality is established. Valid authoritative events that exceed limits must still update the observed ledger and trigger a halt; rejecting their economic existence would understate risk.

Cash expiry/settlement is a separate sourced event identifying full contract, expiry instant, residual signed units, cash basis/value, fee/tax treatment and source version. Verify applicable contract settlement rules (DR-01/06/09) before using it. Cash settlement is not a fill; physical delivery is unsupported until a separate contract exists. Corrected prior-session executions require reopening the relevant accounting lineage, not editing historical rows.

Compare independent order/trade/position snapshots to the gateway at a common complete source watermark (DR-11 Q3). Exact contract/units/order residual agreement is required; no tolerance for quantity mismatch. Unknown external execution must create a quarantine/exposure record even when local OMS has no order. The sampled gateway only accepts executions against known working proposals; a future independent ingestion layer must handle this gap without fabricating local acceptance.

## Q3 — Working-order reservations and lifecycle contract

For known executed position `q_i`, remaining buy units `B_i` and sell units `S_i`, the possible position interval is `[q_i - S_i, q_i + B_i]`. Check both endpoints. Opposite orders cannot be assumed to fill together. Pending and unknown orders reserve all still-possible exposure.

| Lifecycle | Reservation and transition evidence |
|---|---|
| PendingNew | Reserve before any future dispatch. No retry with new identity after uncertain send. Release only proven not sent, or reconciled rejection/terminal outcome. |
| Working / PartiallyFilled | Apply unique fill; reduce residual by exactly that fill; retain remainder. Dedup prevents double position/reservation change. |
| PendingCancel | Keep residual unchanged until confirmed terminal outcome and execution watermark. Cancel request, timeout or disconnect releases nothing. |
| CancelRejected | Keep working/unknown reservation; rejection of cancel does not reject original order. |
| PendingReplace | Preserve old and proposed possible quantities/prices. If exclusive replacement is source-proven, reserve the worst alternative. Otherwise reserve both possible live orders; never assume `max(old,new)` is sufficient for cancel/new emulation. |
| Unknown | Use a justified conservative bound if all possible identities and quantities are known; if unbounded, declare exposure UNKNOWN and block increasing risk. |
| Filled | Position reflects all source-confirmed executions; no order residual. Late correction/bust remains possible and separately processed. |
| Cancelled / Rejected / Expired | Zero residual only after trusted terminal reconciliation with cumulative fills/watermark. Terminal does not erase earlier fills. |
| Risk-reducing close | Reserve closing capacity per instrument as well as possible fills, preventing two concurrent closes from crossing zero. It is not exempt from identity, authenticity, quantity or route validation. |

No reservation is released merely because OMS changed state. The sampled `CancellationEvent` should represent confirmed cancellation, never a request; it cannot encode the pending/unknown distinctions itself.

## Q4 — Proposal checks, formulas and failure behavior

Every decision binds state sequence/hash, policy version, instrument/reference version, time-quality state, source watermarks and evidence references. A later dispatch must revalidate that token against intervening fills, kill state, stale marks and rate budgets. No general-purpose `accepted=true` object is perpetual authority.

| Check | Quantity / rule | Missing or unsupported treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Lots/units | Positive integer lot multiple, dated units-per-lot; per-order cap and both possible-position endpoints | Reject malformed/overflow/unknown contract; do not round requested risk upward |
| Absolute/gross exposure | `G = Σ abs(q_i) abs(mark_i) κ_i + Σ remaining_j max_abs_admissible_price_j κ_j`; use price units compatible with `κ` and source-reviewed fill/valuation bounds | Missing valid marks or unbounded execution price: UNKNOWN, block increasing risk |
| Options underlying exposure | Separately report premium value and `abs(q_i) S_i κ_i`; premium notional is not underlying or loss exposure | Missing underlying/conversion: UNKNOWN, do not substitute premium |
| Concentration | Instrument/underlying/expiry shares against owner-defined denominator and gross cap | Zero/missing denominator is undefined, never zero concentration |
| Margin | Conservative caller-supplied per-unit/contract proxies + explicit scope/date; independent exchange computation is another evidence class | Do not call sum of proxies SPAN/ELM or assume offset credits; missing/stale margin blocks risk increase |
| Loss | Net session realized/unrealized/cost/settlement accounting; equality with hard limit trips persistent latch | Missing mark/fee/cash or reset basis yields UNKNOWN; sampled gateway excludes fees and settlement |
| Stress | Full revaluation over declared scenarios and possible working-order fills, with exit costs; see Q7 | Missing IV, time, curve, settlement convention or unsupported leg: UNKNOWN |
| Greeks | Signed delta/gamma/vega/theta with explicit model/input time and units; gross and net limits separately | Missing is not zero. Hedged net delta alone is insufficient. |
| Rate/OPS | Hierarchical source-reviewed broker/exchange counters and separate local safety policy; include exact documented event classes/scope | Unresolved regulatory counting cannot be certified by a generic per-second bucket; DR-05 dependency |
| Price/session | Dated collar, exchange band, allowed order type, tick, expiry/session eligibility, source freshness | No protection reference or unknown phase means no new risk; no unstated market-order escape |
| Aggregation | Portfolio, strategy, underlying, instrument and all working/reserved proposals checked atomically | Race between two individually passing proposals must not overallocate the shared cap |

Use checked integer arithmetic for units and defined decimal arithmetic for monetary/Greek aggregation. Nonfinite values, negative age, missing currency conversion, wrong unit scale, stale/future marks, invalid negative limits, or unknown source scope are errors. Legitimate zero values (for example a zero option payoff) need field-specific semantics; blanket positivity is not a complete pricing/accounting contract. A sell limit is a minimum execution price, not an upper notional bound; a future adapter needs a reviewed upper absolute price bound from applicable bands/collars or must label its limit-price calculation a proxy. The sampled gateway's limit-price reservation is that narrower proxy.

## Q5 — Meaning of “cancels and risk reduction never blocked”

This is an availability and prioritization invariant, **not permission to bypass correctness**. A risk-limit breach must not itself suppress a valid authenticated cancellation request for an identified order. Provide reserved cancel capacity and a bounded, recorded scheduling deadline compatible with broker limits; duplicate requests are idempotent/coalesced. Rate throttling remains observable. If the route cannot execute, record pending/unavailable, preserve reservation and escalate to the human path; never report completion on intent alone.

A proposed protected close is automatically eligible only if every possible pre-fill position remains on a known side of zero and the aggregate close capacity cannot reverse it. With uncertain position interval `[lo,hi]`, maximum safe SELL close is `max(0,lo)` when `lo>0`; maximum safe BUY close is `max(0,-hi)` when `hi<0`. If the interval spans zero, no nonzero automatic close is proven risk-reducing. First reconcile/cancel outstanding risk, or leave the decision to the authorized human path with explicit uncertainty. Do not infer that an arbitrary hedge reduces all portfolio risks.

Known-side closes may use an explicitly reviewed emergency policy for conservative protection/margin handling; they still require valid identity, quantity, price bounds and evidence that the action reduces the relevant risks. Missing inputs may mean “no safe automated action available,” not a new-risk bypass. K2 requests cancel and protected reduction then halt. K3 isolates a compromised automated route; safe manual controls must be independent. Neither level guarantees flattening during an outage.

The existing closeout exception is narrower: only after daily-loss halt, opposite known position, quantity no greater than it, and no active orders in that instrument; it still requires price/margin and position limits and may exceed gross/margin caps while reserved. It does not implement the broader emergency-lane or uncertain-position protocol above.

## Q6 — Ordering, durability, startup and process contract

1. Assign a durable gateway sequence on ingestion; preserve original source sequence/domain, event UTC, receive UTC and monotonic receive time with boot/process epoch. Do not force externally delayed events into a misleading source chronology by sorting UTC.
2. Use an immutable payload hash for event-ID reuse; scope execution IDs to their source namespace. Record both accepted and rejected decisions, reason and input-state identity if audit completeness is required.
3. Before success, commit the event/reservation and its decision identity durably. On partial/uncertain write, latch unavailable. A readable row alone does not establish acknowledgement durability. Recovery compares to separately controlled receipts; unexplained extra/missing tail blocks automatic continuation.
4. Snapshot includes configuration/reference versions, position/basis/fee state, reservations, execution/correction ledger, latches, rate counters, outstanding decisions and source watermarks. Deterministic replay uses journal time, not current clock, and reproduces all durable decisions. Unsupported versions fail closed.
5. Start SAFE; verify release, acknowledged history and independent reconciliation; explicit authority releases paper activity (DR-11). Session rollover cannot clear operational incidents. The gateway library's arithmetic session reset is not release authorization.
6. A separate process owns state and permission issuance. A watchdog in a separate failure domain monitors it. Missing heartbeat or unavailable gateway prevents increasing-risk dispatch. Enforce fencing/single-writer ownership at the decision consumer; a second process must not use stale permits. Human/manual cancellation remains independently reachable.

Suggested outer state machine: `SAFE -> REPLAY_VERIFIED -> RECONCILED -> READY`; any uncertainty returns to SAFE/RECONCILE. `LOSS_HALTED` and K1–K3 incident latches persist independently of this readiness state until appropriate clearance. `UNAVAILABLE` cannot issue a permit. This outer state is not claimed implemented.

## Q7 — D37 and D38 without owner-selected amounts

Use only explicit units and source inputs. Let `q_i` be signed remaining units, `κ_i` cash multiplier, `V_i(x)` model INR value per unit under scenario `x`, `C_i` signed remaining session basis in INR (positive for long purchase basis, negative for short entry credit; carried positions rebased at approved session opening mark), `R` realized session P&L net of already booked costs, and `H(x)` future exit fees/haircuts in INR. Then:

```text
P_session(x) = R + Σ[q_i κ_i V_i(x) - C_i] - H(x)
loss(x) = max(0, -P_session(x))
required_stress_loss = max over scenarios x and possible working-fill states w of loss(x,w)
accept increasing risk only if required_stress_loss < owner_approved_hard_session_loss
```

For working fills, update cash/basis and q consistently in each fill state; never add a position while omitting its purchase cost. Do not double-count booked costs in `H`. Stress equality trips the proposed hard-loss response, consistently with Q4; this strict boundary is a design recommendation for review. An implementation may use a documented conservative upper bound over working-fill combinations, but may not rely on hedges executing together. Whether realized gains replenish risk budget is a separate owner choice; a conservative alternative caps available room at `max(0, hard_limit + min(0,R))` and measures adverse unbooked loss against that room. Choose one preregistered convention, not whichever passes.

D37's plan examples are spot ±2% and IV ±3 percentage points, four combinations, full option repricing, one tick per option leg and 0.1% futures haircut. They are **unapproved examples, not filled parameters**. IV points mean an absolute volatility-fraction change of 0.03, not 3% of IV. Revaluation needs explicit spot/futures model, rate/dividend/forward treatment, expiry instant, skew/surface shock convention and scenario validity. Black-76 futures-input shifts cannot automatically be inferred from a spot shock.

For D38, let `u` be units per lot; `D` eligible displayed units on the relevant side at one reviewed snapshot; `α` owner-selected depth fraction; `p` a reviewed upper bound on admissible option execution premium in INR/unit (not a sell-limit floor); `Pcap` owner premium INR cap; `d` absolute per-unit delta in the chosen convention; `Lref` underlying units per reference NIFTY lot; `Dcap` allowed delta-equivalent lots. For a plain option with unit cash multiplier one:

```text
n_depth = floor(α D / u)
n_premium = floor(Pcap / (p u))
n_delta = floor(Dcap Lref / (d u))
maximum_lots = min(n_depth, n_premium, n_delta, other_reviewed_limits)
```

General multipliers/conversions must be included explicitly. D38's quarter-depth, INR32,500 and 1.5 delta lots are examples only. If valid `d=0`, the delta-only bound is nonbinding, while depth/premium/stress limits still bind; unknown delta cannot be treated as zero. `p=0` needs explicit price/payoff policy, not division by zero. Deduct already reserved depth/capacity and enforce portfolio aggregate; two proposals cannot each reuse the same displayed-size allowance. Displayed depth is a snapshot assumption, not executable capacity (DR-08).
