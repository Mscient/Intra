## Answers to the six brief questions

### 1. Components, bases, sides and events

The source register below is the authoritative statement of researched values. Units are INR, with one crore = 10,000,000 INR; one basis point = 0.01%. Futures execution turnover is price times underlying units; options execution turnover is premium times units. Lot size must come from the dated contract master, not from this note.

| Component | Execution scope | Expiry/exercise scope | Research conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brokerage | Account tariff; advertised fixed charge is per executed order, not automatically per fill | Explicit conflict C3 below | No account default approved |
| Exchange transaction charge and IPFT | Each executed side; futures value or option premium | No complete direct expiry charge authority established | Keep separate components even when totals are unchanged |
| SEBI turnover fee | Futures/options turnover, per source S6 | Regulation expressly includes expiry turnover and additional exercised/assigned option notional | Exact per-unit expiry basis and client pass-through still required |
| STT | Seller on futures trade value or option premium | Exercised option purchaser; intrinsic-value basis | Separate TRADE and EXERCISE events; short assignment is not automatically purchaser STT |
| Stamp duty | Buyer; futures turnover or option premium | No separate NIFTY cash-expiry duty established here | Do not invent an expiry zero or duplicate trade duty |
| GST | Brokerage/service fee base | Service character and invoice classification needed | STT/stamp exclusion is conditional on pure-agent requirements |
| Other | Call-and-trade, funding, collateral, delayed payment, administrative charges can be separate tariff events | Penalties, delivery and broker handling require exact product/event scope | No universal all-in amount or exhaustive zero declaration |

### 2. Effective dates, rounding, caps and exemptions

The CSV uses **inclusive effective_from and inclusive effective_through**. A blank date is unknown/open in the source, **not permission to use the row indefinitely**. Every row is `PENDING_REVIEW`, and `production_eligible=false`. Current pages are observed on the access date; no earliest history is inferred from an update timestamp. Through dates closed by an identified superseding circular are distinguished from an unverified amendment horizon.

Known dated transitions: STT trade rates from 2024-10-01 to 2026-03-31; replacement from 2026-04-01; exchange/IPFT split from 2026-03-01; stamp collection regime from 2020-07-01; 2026 broker-regulation source from Gazette publication 2026-01-07. Earlier transaction-charge history remains incomplete because the cited NSE/FA/64323 original was not accessible through the research tool. Its old values are visible in S1's comparison table, but that table does not independently establish the entire earlier effective period.

**Rounding is a blocker.** The reviewed sources do not establish a complete NIFTY/Angel component-by-component rounding contract: per trade, per order, per contract/day or client/day; paise versus whole rupee; tie rule; rounding before GST; tax invoice reconciliation. No universal minimum, maximum or exemption was established for each levy. Do not encode missing caps as zero. The dated NIFTYFPI introductory waiver on Angel's page concerns a different index and must not be applied to NIFTY 50.

### 3. Broker square-off, expiry and additional charges

S8's auto-square-off support answer quotes INR 20 per executed order; S7 describes it as the normal brokerage with no additional penalty. Treat these as published descriptions, not a reason to double-charge INR 20 brokerage plus another INR 20 square-off fee. Plan, date, tax and order aggregation require the owner's tariff. Call-and-trade, clearing, administrative, funding and default events require separate event IDs and account evidence. Index cash settlement must not inherit single-stock physical-delivery, auction, demat or delivery-margin charges. No physical-settlement charge table is proposed for NIFTY. Whether an additional clearing/settlement charge is legally and contractually applicable remains unresolved; absence of a researched tariff is not proof of zero.

### 5. GST treatment

S7 describes its ordinary taxable base as brokerage + exchange + IPFT + SEBI fees. S5 confirms brokerage taxability and conditional pure-agent exclusions for recovered taxes. This is enough for a **candidate** base, not invoice-specific approval. The historical rate commencement, present notification/SAC, CGST/SGST versus IGST, minimum taxable amounts, input tax credit, rounding, and treatment of disputed expiry service charges require accountant/reviewer evidence. Do not include tax credit in strategy P&L by assumption. Future tax-schedule rows need separate `taxable_component_set`, jurisdiction, notification reference, invoice aggregation and rounding fields.

Current local sources inspected: `provenance/CostSchedule.java`, `ComponentCostSchedule.java`, `SettlementCostSchedule.java`; `simulation/portfolio/PortfolioExecutionCostSchedule.java`, `PortfolioSettlementCostSchedule.java`; current [portfolio settlement contract](../../../Intra-Paper-System/docs/PORTFOLIO-SETTLEMENT.md). The implementation has advanced beyond the brief's older single-contract snapshot; signed portfolio settlements and caller-declared charge/GST rounding already exist. This assignment makes no code changes and no claim about unrelated dirty-worktree checks.

`ComponentCostSchedule.Terms.calculate` and portfolio execution terms round components to paise HALF_UP and add fixed/per-unit/bps brokerage components. That structure cannot by itself establish a broker minimum/maximum tariff or client/day aggregation. Preserve a future order-level fee accumulator and invoice reconciliation boundary where needed, rather than invoking fixed-order brokerage per fill. Existing single-contract inclusive `effective_through` and portfolio exclusive `effective_until` require an explicit reviewed conversion, never a copied same endpoint date.

Settlement rows need event kind, signed position side, sourced exercise classification, separate intrinsic and SEBI-notional bases, source document and pinpoint, account tariff, rounding level/mode/scale, taxable-component set, and review identity. A fee component declared only by code cannot prove externally complete coverage. Missing applicable source, unresolved conflict, or unverified account scope must keep cost evidence unverified. Hashes establish byte identity, not issuer authenticity or legal completeness.
