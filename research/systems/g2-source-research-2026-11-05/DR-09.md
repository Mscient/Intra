Researcher: Codex, mechanics/model/feed assignment. Research/access date: **2026-11-05**, Asia/Kolkata. Scope: NSE NIFTY index options, their spot/forward inputs, and existing diagnostic Java pricers. **Research returned; pending independent source and input review.** No source-derived production value is approved.

## Executive finding

The R11/QuantLib comparison already establishes a scoped numerical implementation result. It does not establish a usable NIFTY quote surface, an expiry clock, approved rates/carry, or settlement correctness. This review also found a material primary-source conflict: the current NSE NIFTY product page says “physical settled” for its index options, while NSE Clearing's settlement-mechanism page describes cash exercise settlement. This is recorded as a conflict, not silently corrected or promoted into a registry. Dated circular/master evidence and independent review must resolve the applicable contract convention.

NIFTY OPTIDX is an option on the index. Selecting the program's `BLACK76_FUTURE` enum does not make it a listed option on a NIFTY futures contract. A forward-input representation of an index option needs a justified same-expiry forward and discount convention. No futures-option contract in this project has been independently identified as an approved tradable instrument.

## Conventions — question 1

Treat `exercise_style`, `settlement_type`, `expiry_local_date`, `last_trade_instant`, `payoff_reference_instant`, `settlement_cash_date`, `holiday_calendar_version`, `contract_multiplier`, `market_lot`, `tick_size`, `underlying_id` and `settlement_reference_rule` as separately sourced fields. V1–V4 are candidate evidence; V2 blocks unqualified settlement-convention approval. DR-01 must establish date-specific expiry and final session boundaries, including holiday transitions and CAS amendments. An end-of-day date cannot substitute for a precise expiry instant.

No primary exchange rule prescribing ACT/365 as a universal valuation day count was established. Current Java receives a caller-supplied fractional year; its “typically ACT/365” comment is not an exchange mandate. Proposed model configuration must separately specify volatility year basis, elapsed calendar-time fraction, rate compounding/day count, discount factor and cash-payment lag. Do not silently truncate to trading days, add an overnight day, or treat `T=0` reporting conventions as a risk limit. Broker square-off/expiry restrictions require account-product evidence and do not redefine contractual expiry.

## Quote eligibility — question 3

These are proposed research decisions, not exchange-certified numeric thresholds. Owner-configured freshness, leg-skew, maximum spread and outlier tolerances remain **unset**, versioned and labeled provisional until evidence supports them.

| Condition | Proposed eligibility/result |
|---|---|
| Bid exceeds ask; nonfinite/negative price or size | Quarantine observation with original bytes; never repair by swapping sides |
| Locked quote | Preserve and flag; no automatic midpoint-IV eligibility without source/phase review |
| Zero bid, nonzero ask | May provide an upper pricing interval; no forced midpoint IV or sale fill at a positive invented bid |
| Stale underlying, asynchronous option/forward, uncertain clocks | Ineligible for synchronized point calibration; record each age/skew and uncertainty |
| Wide spread | Retain interval; compare model residual to bid/ask and ticks, not only midpoint error |
| Auction/indicative value | Separate phase; do not mix with continuous executable quote fit |
| Outlier | Flag with rule and source lineage; no automatic deletion based on inconvenient fit residual |
| Missing strike/expiry | Explicit coverage hole; no fabricated quoted observation |
| Duplicate/revised event | Preserve identity/revision; distinguish duplicate bytes from equal-time independent updates |
| At/after expiry | Stop pre-expiry IV inference; use approved settlement event for accounting, not a model guess |

## Model choice and Greek units — question 4

Local-code review: BSM takes positive spot/strike, continuously compounded annual decimal `r` and `q`, positive annual volatility and nonnegative supplied `T`. Black-76 takes positive forward/futures level, discount rate and `T`, holding that level fixed for rate/theta sensitivities. The valuation CSV requires blank dividend yield for `BLACK76_FUTURE`. The arithmetic relation `F=S exp((r-q)T)` can make the two prices agree under consistent assumptions; it does not establish that observed futures basis, payoff fixing and discount horizon satisfy those assumptions.

Current output convention: price per underlying unit; delta per one-unit spot movement (BSM) or forward movement (Black-76); gamma per squared underlying-price unit; vega per **0.01** absolute volatility change; rho per **0.01** absolute rate change; theta per 365-calendar day. Position-value sensitivities multiply by signed units (`contracts × dated lot size`) once, with currency/index-point units displayed. Vega “one point” must not be confused with a 1% relative change in volatility. Forward delta and spot delta cannot be directly netted without a sourced conversion model; fixed-forward rho is not BSM rho. At expiry, Java reports half-delta ATM and zero gamma by convention; ATM expiry gamma is singular, so the displayed zero is not proof of zero risk.

## Surface support and limits — question 5

**Not supportable from evidence presently supplied.** Mathematical diagnostics need at least two distinct strikes for a vertical-spread comparison, three for curvature, and two compatible maturities for a calendar comparison. These are logical minima, not sufficient calibration coverage or mandated counts. Each slice needs eligible observations around the intended moneyness domain, both wings where risk is evaluated, multiple expiry horizons where interpolation is proposed, synchronized forward/discount inputs and holdout observations independent of fit selection. No numeric sample minimum is selected without the chosen model and empirical coverage.

For European calls with positive discount factor `D` and a consistent forward, own-arithmetic checks include `D max(F-K,0) ≤ C ≤ D F`, put counterpart `D max(K-F,0) ≤ P ≤ D K`, parity `C-P=D(F-K)`, nonincreasing call prices in strike and nonnegative divided second differences for nonuniform strike grids. Apply tolerances to feasible bid/ask intervals, not only to noisy midpoints. Negative rates can make `D>1`; bounds must use actual discounting. Across expiry, do not compare raw premiums naively when carry, settlement times or underlying forwards differ; V6's total-variance condition requires its stated assumptions.

Interpolation must be confined to an explicitly supported region and rechecked for static arbitrage; no uncontrolled wing or expiry extrapolation. Sparse slices remain sparse. Proposed validation metrics: eligible coverage by strike/expiry/phase; price error in ticks and spread units; bid/ask containment; constraint violations; leave-strike/expiry-out performance; perturbation stability; Greek/IV sensitivity to spread and input timing; and rejected-observation reasons. No calibration family is implemented or selected here.

## Review gate and current code consequences — question 7

`data/OptionValuationInputSchedule.java` matches one input to each option bar's computed close instant and requires a source string; it does not establish underlying freshness, day-count correctness, licensed rights, or source authenticity. Proposed richer input validation should sit before that adapter. `pricing/EuropeanOptionPricer.java` and `Black76OptionPricer.java` numerical behavior need not be reimplemented. `simulation/OptionValuationAnalyzer.java`, `OptionStressRiskGate.java` and portfolio risk/stress consumers need explicit eligibility, convention and Greek-unit evidence before production use. Existing settlement accounting and independent risk modules do not remove these source gates.

Required independent review: contract identity and V2 conflict; expiry/fixing/payment clocks; source rights and timestamp semantics; forward/rates/carry choice; quote cleaning and sparse-domain policy; unit conversions and position aggregation; model-risk/residual stress and source-update controls. Then separately review accounting/fee/margin integration. Exchange margin is not a model-Greek formula, and theoretical value is not a statutory charge base. Until that review, model values remain diagnostic and cannot establish account-level safety or source-correct fees/P&L/margin.
