Never make cancellation erase earlier fills. A cancel request is not cancel effectiveness. Quantity-decrease priority retention, quantity-increase reset and price-change reset are **not confirmed parameter rules here**; keep the toy book's choices explicitly exchange-neutral. Exact freeze numbers, LPP ranges, stop-market availability and Angel risk filters remain owner/source blockers.

## Queue identification and fill tiers — questions 3 and 5

The following are **INFERENCE / proposed research contracts**, not exchange promises. Two paths can have identical snapshots: quantity decreases because trades consume orders ahead, or because orders behind cancel while the front persists. New orders and replenishment can occur between observations. Thus neither event order nor a positive passive fill lower bound is identified. With snapshots alone, the only universally conservative lower bound is **zero passive fill**; “assume cancellations behind us” is a scenario, not a certified bound when hidden size, omitted trades and priority are unknown.

| Tier | Required evidence | Calibration and principal bias | Validation / permitted claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Next-bar price | Dated bars, known label, calendar and costs | No intrabar path, spread, latency or capacity observed | No same-decision-bar execution; diagnostic next-open scenario |
| Crossing L1 IOC | Causally available eligible quote, size/units, explicit latency scenario | Snapshot may be stale when hypothetical order arrives; repeated snapshots can double-count persistent liquidity | Whole-lot top-size cap, residual cancellation, quote-consumption reconciliation; conditional displayed-size simulation only |
| Conservative passive scenario | Complete trade observations, depth and explicit queue assumptions | Cancellation location/hidden quantity unknown; positive bound unjustified with snapshots alone | Report zero certified lower bound separately from assumption-driven fill scenario |
| Probabilistic queue estimate | Authorized granular events, independently checked outcomes, representative sessions | Selection bias, nonstationarity, dependence and censored outcomes | Out-of-sample probability calibration and uncertainty by regime; currently blocked, no guessed probabilities |
| Order-event reconstruction | Complete initial state/events, order IDs, stream/tie rules and amendments | Missing implied/hidden behavior and historical counterfactual impact can remain | Reconcile reconstructed book/trades and all gaps; reconstructed historical queue is not guaranteed hypothetical own-order execution |

Do not treat synthetic fixtures as calibration or use the plan's illustrative fraction-of-depth cap as evidence. A model can be conservative in one dimension and optimistic in another.

## Latency and capacity — questions 6 and 7

Read-only measurements can cover local decode/processing/write intervals, inter-arrival silence and lifecycle reconnect durations. Source timestamp to local wall time is only an observed age with clock/semantic uncertainty, not order latency. Transmit-to-ack, exchange admission, cancel-effective latency and fill notification timing require actual order-path evidence, which this assignment does not request or authorize. DR-10 gives the clock contract.

Displayed size is a snapshot condition, executed volume is a flow, hidden liquidity is unobserved supply, and sustainable participation is a response of the market to trading. They cannot substitute for one another. Capacity requires permitted volume/depth, event timing, competing-flow and impact evidence, plus sensitivity to participation and adverse selection. No impact coefficient or sustainable size is inferred here. Repeated independent replays must not be summed as if each can consume the same liquidity without competition.
