## Q2 — Margin components and dated distinctions

M1's simplified accounting is `SPAN margin = scan risk + intra-commodity spread risk - net option value`, subject to the full applicable method. It is not an independent complete calculator. A portfolio's worst scenario differs from a sum of per-contract maxima. A generic BSM/Black-76 stress grid is not the exchange risk array. ELM, payable obligations and applicable additions must remain distinct; reporting only SPAN understates the required evidence.

M2's current page describes ordinary index ELM at 2% of notional, short deep-OTM index options at 3%, and long-dated short index options (>9 months) at 5%. These are **current-page observations with unknown historical commencement**, not dated tariff rows. Notional bases differ for futures and short options. Do not resolve overlapping classifications, special add-ons or rounding from these prose summaries. For the dated M4 change, use the contract file's final ELM percentage once; it already includes the expiry addition.

Index expiry spread withdrawal has a specific scope/date in M6/M9; it does not abolish all offsets between unexpired legs or stock-derivative rules. M3's generic through-expiry sentence is inadequate for post-change index positions. Premium availability for an option purchase is also not equivalent to “zero required funds.” M5 establishes the postponement only; exact gross/net and release semantics require the predecessor method and broker evidence.

Delivery/physical-settlement requirements concern a different event/product from assumed NIFTY cash settlement. Their applicability is unresolved until DR01/09 resolves dated settlement conventions; do not import single-stock delivery margins into NIFTY. Broker house buffers, cash/collateral allocation, funding and liquidation can differ from the CC model. “Peak margin” is not a universal multiplier: M7/M8 require a distinct client-verification computation, independent of the CC's current obligation.

## Q4 — Missingness, updates and edge treatment

All following controls are **recommended fail-closed design**, not additional exchange requirements.

| Condition | Deterministic treatment |
|---|---|
| Missing/late/corrupt file or required field | Return `MARGIN_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`; block risk increase; preserve original evidence and reason. Never use zero. |
| Parameter update arrives after decision | Earlier run uses only previously available data; later correction creates a separately versioned research result. No hindsight rewrite. |
| BOD versus intraday file | Select using declared calculation purpose and regime; never substitute intraday CC file into BOD verification or vice versa. |
| Lot/expiry/token change | Require exact dated identity/conversion; reject ambiguous mapping and mismatched contract key. |
| Expiry or holiday | Use reviewed actual expiry/session calendar; stop new position calculations after validity ends, but retain unsettled obligations until evidenced release. |
| Offsetting positions | Net only within permitted client/proprietary set; no cross-client netting. Require spread eligibility for each leg on that instant. |
| Broker multiplier/buffer absent | Report broker margin UNKNOWN. Do not invent a house factor or treat exchange figure as buying power. |
| Negative/unparseable quantity or arithmetic overflow | Signed positions allowed, invalid rate/scale rejected; checked arithmetic/decimal precision; no wraparound. |
| Rounding unspecified | Produce raw diagnostic only; no paise tie-out accepted until rounding and comparison policy approved. |
